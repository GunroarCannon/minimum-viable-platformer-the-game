Privacy Policy

Minimum Viable Platformer · Critbox Software · Effective 28 September 2026

This policy explains what information the Minimum Viable Platformer game ("the game") collects, why, and what you can do about it. The short version: the game collects only what it needs for the online leaderboards and for in-app purchases. It has no ads and no analytics or tracking, and it never sells your data.

What we collect

DataWhyWhere it goes
A player ID, derived from your device's app identifier To keep your leaderboard entry and purchases tied to you, including after a reinstall Google Firebase (Firestore) and RevenueCat
The display name you choose Shown next to your scores on the public leaderboards Google Firebase (Firestore)
Scores: distance, points, combo Leaderboards and the Daily Level Google Firebase (Firestore)
Purchase history To deliver and restore what you bought Google Play and RevenueCat

The display name and scores you submit are public: anyone playing the game can see them on the leaderboards. Please don't use your real name or personal details as a display name.

What we don't collect

  • No advertising, and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, crash-tracking or behaviour-tracking SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or files.
  • No payment details. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play, so we never see your card or account.

Data stored only on your device

Your game progress, unlocked upgrades, tokens, statistics and settings are saved locally on your device. They are not uploaded, and uninstalling the game deletes them.

Third-party services

  • Google Firebase / Cloud Firestore stores the leaderboards. Firebase privacy information
  • RevenueCat manages in-app purchases. RevenueCat privacy policy
  • Google Play processes payments. Google privacy policy

We share data with these services only so they can provide these features. We do not sell or rent personal data to anyone.

Security and retention

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboards run. Purchase records are kept as long as needed to honour and restore your purchases and to meet legal and accounting obligations.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • You can play without ever submitting a score. The leaderboard is an optional unlock.
  • To have your leaderboard entry and player ID deleted, email us at gunroarcannon@gmail.com with your display name. We'll delete it within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data. Use the same email for any such request.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has submitted information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
